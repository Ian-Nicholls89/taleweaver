# Rules quick reference (SRD 5.2)

## Core d20 test
- Ability check, attack roll or saving throw: d20 + ability modifier (+ proficiency bonus if proficient) vs a target number.
- Difficulty Classes: very easy 5, easy 10, medium 15, hard 20, very hard 25, nearly impossible 30.
- Advantage: roll two d20s, keep the higher. Disadvantage: keep the lower. They cancel out; multiple sources don't stack.
- A natural 20 on an attack roll always hits and is a critical hit (roll the damage dice twice). A natural 1 always misses. Natural 20/1 have no special effect on checks and saves.
- Heroic Inspiration: the player may spend it to reroll any die once.
- Only call for a roll when failure is possible and interesting. Otherwise, the character simply succeeds.
- Passive Perception = 10 + Perception bonus, for noticing hidden things without an active search.

## Abilities and skills
- Strength: Athletics. Dexterity: Acrobatics, Sleight of Hand, Stealth. Intelligence: Arcana, History, Investigation, Nature, Religion. Wisdom: Animal Handling, Insight, Medicine, Perception, Survival. Charisma: Deception, Intimidation, Performance, Persuasion.
- Saving throws resist harmful effects: Dexterity to dodge area effects, Constitution to endure poison or keep concentration, Wisdom against charm and fear, Strength against being shoved or restrained.

## Combat
- Initiative: each combatant rolls a Dexterity check; act in descending order. For a solo game, it is fine to resolve initiative narratively and alternate between the hero and enemy groups.
- On their turn a creature can move up to its speed and take one action: Attack, Cast a Spell, Dash, Disengage, Dodge, Help, Hide, Influence, Magic (use a magic item), Ready, Search, Study, or Utilize (use an object). Some features grant a bonus action. One reaction per round (e.g. an opportunity attack when an enemy leaves reach).
- Attack roll vs Armour Class. Damage = weapon or spell dice + ability modifier (for weapons).
- Cover: half cover +2 AC, three-quarters cover +5 AC, total cover can't be targeted.
- Ranged attacks within 5 feet of an enemy, or at long range, have disadvantage. Attacks against a prone target have advantage within 5 feet, disadvantage from farther away.
- Unseen attackers have advantage; attacks against a target you can't see have disadvantage.
- Grapple or shove: the target makes a Strength or Dexterity save against DC 8 + attacker's Strength modifier + proficiency bonus.

## Hit points, dying and resting
- At 0 HP a character falls unconscious and makes a death saving throw at the start of each of their turns: d20, 10+ success, below 10 failure. Three successes: stable. Three failures: dead. Natural 20: regain 1 HP. Natural 1: two failures. Taking damage at 0 HP causes a failure (a critical hit causes two).
- Massive damage: if damage at 0 HP equals or exceeds the character's HP maximum, they die outright.
- Any healing brings a creature back to consciousness.
- Short rest (1 hour): spend Hit Dice to heal (roll the class die + Con modifier each). Long rest (8 hours): regain all HP, half the Hit Dice, and spell slots.
- Potion of Healing: drink as a bonus action, regain 2d4 + 2 HP.

## Spellcasting
- Spell attack bonus = spellcasting ability modifier + proficiency bonus. Spell save DC = 8 + spellcasting modifier + proficiency bonus.
- Casting a levelled spell uses a spell slot of that level or higher. Cantrips are free.
- Concentration: only one concentration spell at a time; taking damage requires a Constitution save (DC 10 or half the damage, whichever is higher, max DC 30) to keep it.

## Conditions
Blinded (fails sight checks; its attacks have disadvantage, attacks against it have advantage). Charmed (can't attack the charmer; charmer has advantage on social checks). Deafened. Frightened (disadvantage on checks and attacks while the source is in sight; can't move closer). Grappled (speed 0). Incapacitated (no actions or reactions). Invisible (attacks against it have disadvantage, its attacks have advantage). Paralysed (incapacitated, fails Str/Dex saves, hits within 5 ft are critical). Petrified. Poisoned (disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks). Prone (crawl; disadvantage on attacks; see above for attacks against). Restrained (speed 0, disadvantage on attacks and Dex saves, attacks against it have advantage). Stunned (incapacitated, fails Str/Dex saves, attacks against it have advantage). Unconscious (incapacitated, prone, drops what it holds; hits within 5 ft are critical). Exhaustion (each level: −2 to d20 tests, −5 ft speed; 6 levels is death).

## Encounter guidance for one hero
- Low-level solo heroes are fragile. A fair fight at level 1 is one or two weak foes (CR 1/8–1/4), or one CR 1/2 foe. At level 3, up to a CR 1 foe with a minion or two.
- Let enemies behave sensibly: many flee or surrender when badly hurt. Offer non-combat solutions.
- If a fight is going badly, allow a retreat, capture, or rescue instead of a pointless death — unless the player is clearly courting it.
