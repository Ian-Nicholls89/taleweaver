## Overview
A light, comedic one-shot where talking, trickery and bravado matter more than swords. **Chief Snagtooth**, a small goblin with big ambitions, has blocked the pass with a rickety toll-gate and a dozen nervous goblins. He wants a hat "fit for a king" because he is holding a coronation — his own — at the next full moon. A merchant caravan (led by the anxious **Madame Pell**) can't get through.

The hero can find, make, steal, or fake a legendary hat, or topple Snagtooth some other way. Violence is possible but the goblins mostly run away; the fun is in the scheming.

## Hook
At the foot of Knuckle Pass the hero finds six wagons circled and Madame Pell shouting at a goblin herald holding a sign: "TOL: 1 HAT (GUD ONE)". Pell offers 40 gp and a pick from her cargo to anyone who can get the caravan through before her cheese spoils.

## Scenes

### 1. The toll-gate
A gate of sticks and a barrel, guarded by **Grib** and **Nubb** (goblins, bickering). They escort the hero up to meet the chief in a cave "palace" decorated with stolen tapestries.
- **Chief Snagtooth** sits on a throne of saddles. He is pompous, insecure, and keen on "proper royal things". He rejects any ordinary hat with great drama ("This is a hat for a FARMER").
- He'll describe the hat he wants: "tall, shiny, with a feather from a bird that TALKS, and it must have been worn by someone IMPORTANT".
- Insight DC 12: Snagtooth is terrified that his goblins don't respect him. The hat is about respect.

### 2. The quest for the hat (hero chooses; any order)
- **The talking bird**: an old parrot, **Captain Squall**, belongs to a retired sailor, **Dunmore**, in the waystation inn at the bottom of the pass. The parrot insults everyone. Persuasion DC 13 (or doing Dunmore a favour — fixing his leaky roof, winning a game of dice) gets a tail feather. Sleight of Hand DC 14 to pluck one; Squall screams "THIEF! THIEF!" on a failure.
- **Someone important's hat**: Madame Pell's cargo includes a battered tricorn that she claims belonged to a famous admiral (it didn't — Investigation DC 12 reveals a tailor's tag from last year). A good liar can sell it anyway.
- **Something shiny**: a nest of **giant magpies** on the cliff (use Kobold stats as swooping birds, 7 HP, flying, or simply Athletics DC 12 to climb and Dexterity saves DC 11 to avoid being pecked for 1d4). The nest holds brass buttons, a silver spoon, and a gold-plated circlet.
- **Making it**: Madame Pell's wagons include felt, glue, and a milliner's kit. An hour's work and a DC 12 Dexterity or Intelligence check (the hero can use ingenuity to gain advantage) turns the pieces into a magnificent hat.

### 3. Rival claimant
While the hero is hat-hunting, **Grizzek**, Snagtooth's big and ambitious lieutenant (use Goblin Boss-like stats: AC 15, 18 HP, scimitar +4, 1d6+2), tries to sabotage the hero's quest — he wants to be chief himself. He might try to steal the hat, or ambush the hero with two goblins (Goblin Warrior, 7 HP each, they flee when one drops).
- A clever hero may turn Grizzek into an ally or expose him in front of the tribe.

### 4. The coronation
At dusk the goblins gather. The hero presents the hat. Performance or Persuasion DC 13 to make the presentation grand enough (advantage if the hat has all three qualities). Snagtooth is overjoyed — unless Grizzek interferes, in which case a comic duel, a battle of insults, or a well-timed revelation decides who gets crowned.

## NPCs
- **Chief Snagtooth** — tiny, pompous, speaks of himself in the third person, secretly lonely.
- **Grizzek** — muscular, sneering, not clever. Wants the throne. Can be bribed with a better hat (irony!).
- **Grib and Nubb** — goblin guards, a double act; Grib is eager, Nubb is lazy.
- **Madame Pell** — cheesemonger, frantic, obsessed with the ripeness of her wheels of cheese.
- **Dunmore** — retired sailor with a wooden leg and endless tall tales.
- **Captain Squall** — a parrot who insults people with startling accuracy.

## Encounters
- Giant magpies (optional hazard or light fight).
- Grizzek and two goblin warriors (easy to defuse).
- Most challenges are skill checks and roleplay.

## Secrets & Clues
- Snagtooth's toll-gate is illegal, but the local lord doesn't care to send soldiers for goblins.
- Grizzek has been stealing from the tribe's stores and blaming the caravans.
- The "admiral's hat" is a fake — but Snagtooth will never know unless someone tells him.

## Possible Endings
- **Long live King Snagtooth**: the pass opens; Snagtooth declares the hero "Royal Hat Knight" and offers future safe passage. Pell pays up and gives the hero a wheel of fine cheese (worth 10 gp, smells worth 1 cp).
- **Grizzek's coup**: if the hero sides with Grizzek, the pass opens, but it's clear he'll demand far worse tolls in future.
- **The goblins scatter**: a violent solution works, but the hero is left with a hat nobody wanted and a cave full of saddles.
