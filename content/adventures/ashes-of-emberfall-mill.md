## Overview
**Aldric Thorne**, the miller, died in the fire that destroyed Emberfall Mill. The fire was not an accident: his business rival, **Garrett Ambry**, now the wealthy owner of the new mill in town, set it. Aldric's grief and rage kindled into a small fire spirit (an SRD **Magma Mephit**-like elemental, flavoured as a "cinder wisp") bound to the ruins. At first it only baked bread for the village as the miller always had. Now Garrett is planning to build over the ruins, and the spirit's anger is spilling into fires.

The hero must uncover the truth. The spirit can be laid to rest by exposing Garrett's crime, or destroyed in a fiery confrontation.

## Hook
The hero is passing through Hollin when a barn goes up in flames. They help with the bucket line, and afterwards **Reeve Martha Cobb** hires them (30 gp) to "find out what's haunting the old mill before it burns the whole village down".

## Scenes

### 1. Hollin after the fire
A quiet farming village of thatched roofs by a slow river. Smoke still curls from the burned barn.
- **Reeve Cobb** (practical, blunt) explains the history: the mill fire, the smoke, the bread, and that the barn belonged to Garrett Ambry.
- **Tobin Thorne**, Aldric's grown son, now a tanner, insists his father "would never hurt a soul". He still keeps his father's journal but hasn't been able to read it since the fire.
- **Garrett Ambry** arrives with two hired guards, blustering about "superstitious nonsense", and announces he'll clear the ruins tomorrow.
- A DC 13 Insight check on Garrett reveals he is frightened, not angry.

### 2. Tobin's workshop and the journal
- The journal (if Tobin lends it — Persuasion DC 10, or automatically if the hero is kind) describes Garrett's threats ("sell to me or regret it") in the weeks before the fire.
- The last page mentions a hidden strongbox under the millstone where Aldric kept his deeds and savings.

### 3. The ruins at night
Blackened timbers, a broken waterwheel creaking in the river, a warm glow in the cellar. The smell of fresh bread.
- Survival or Perception DC 12: tiny scorched footprints leading to the old oven.
- The **cinder wisp** appears as a flickering figure of an old man made of embers. It does not attack unless attacked or unless Garrett is mentioned kindly. It speaks in crackles and single words: "Bread. Warm. Ambry. LIAR."
- If the hero tries to talk, Persuasion or Religion DC 12 calms it enough to ask questions (it can point, nod, and show embers forming images).
- If provoked, the wisp attacks: AC 11, 22 HP, Fiery Touch +3, 1d6+1 fire, and once per fight a **Cinder Burst** (DC 11 Dex save, 2d6 fire in a 10-foot radius, half on success). It takes 1d4 damage from each gallon of water thrown on it.

### 4. The strongbox under the millstone
Moving the fallen millstone needs Athletics DC 14 (or a lever, a rope and the waterwheel — let clever ideas work). Inside: the mill deed, 40 gp, and a letter from Garrett offering to buy the mill "before something unfortunate happens".

### 5. Confrontation
Garrett, learning the hero is poking around, comes to the ruins at dawn with his two guards (use Bandit stats, 11 HP each) and a cask of lamp oil to "finish the job".
- If the hero reveals the letter publicly, or tricks Garrett into confessing in front of the spirit or the Reeve, the spirit's rage turns to relief.
- If the hero fights, the guards flee when one falls or when the spirit appears.
- If the hero sides with Garrett, the spirit grows into a blaze (add 10 HP, Cinder Burst recharges on 5–6) and the fight is dangerous.

## NPCs
- **Reeve Martha Cobb** — no-nonsense, cares about the village above all.
- **Tobin Thorne** — gentle, grieving, desperate to clear his father's name.
- **Garrett Ambry** — wealthy, well-dressed, a coward rather than a monster; he has had nightmares for twenty years.
- **The cinder wisp** — the smouldering remnant of Aldric; confused, grieving, dangerous when angered.

## Encounters
- The cinder wisp (optional fight).
- Garrett's guards ×2.
- Hazards: unstable ruins (Dex save DC 11 to avoid a falling beam, 1d6), the river (Athletics DC 10 to cross at the weir).

## Secrets & Clues
- The bread is baked with flour from the old mill's stores — someone should have noticed it never runs out.
- Garrett's barn was the only building to burn; the spirit is targeting him.
- The letter in the strongbox proves the threat; Garrett's confession proves the crime.

## Possible Endings
- **Laid to rest**: Garrett confesses before the Reeve. The spirit bakes one last loaf, places it in Tobin's hands, and fades into warm light. Tobin gives the hero his father's lucky flour scoop (a trinket), plus the reward.
- **Extinguished**: the spirit is destroyed; the fires stop, but Tobin is heartbroken and Garrett goes unpunished unless the hero exposes him later.
- **Burned**: if the hero fails, the mill fire spreads to Garrett's new mill; the village survives but blames the stranger.
