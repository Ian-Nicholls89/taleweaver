## Overview
The Night Market is run by fey merchants who trade in intangible things. Its master is **the Hollow Auctioneer**, a genteel fey noble who collects names. The hero's friend **Liss Marrow** (a street musician) traded her name for a song of perfect beauty, not realising what it meant. Without her name she is fading; by the next moonrise she'll be forgotten by everyone.

The hero must navigate the market's strange rules, find where Liss's name is being sold, and win it back through a bargain, a trick, a contest or theft. Fey magic means violence is dangerous and bargains are binding.

## Hook
The hero finds Liss sitting on a doorstep, humming a beautiful tune and unable to remember who she is. People walk past her as if she isn't there. On her wrist is a silver receipt: "ONE NAME, RECEIVED WITH THANKS. QUILL STREET MARKET." At midnight, a narrow street lined with lanterns appears between two buildings where there was only a wall.

## The market's rules (tell the hero when they arrive — a gnome crier shouts them)
1. Nothing is free, but everything is for sale.
2. No steel may be drawn (drawing a metal weapon makes all merchants hostile and the market closes around you — a DC 13 Wisdom save or be charmed and thrown out).
3. A deal spoken is a deal kept.
4. Leave before the last lantern goes out at dawn, or stay forever.

## Scenes

### 1. Entering the market
Lanterns in a hundred colours; the smell of honey, ozone and old books; music with no source. Stalls include:
- **Mother Umber**, who sells bottled weather ("a sunny afternoon, 1 memory of a birthday").
- **Pickle and Wick**, twin pixies who sell lucky shadows (a shadow gives advantage on one Stealth check).
- **The Sighing Stall**, which buys and sells regrets.
- Currency: memories, secrets, a year of your life, your laughter for a day, true answers. Gold is accepted but considered vulgar (prices triple).
- Insight DC 12 on any merchant reveals they're amused by mortals but bound to honour deals exactly as spoken.

### 2. Tracking the name
- Asking around: most merchants deflect. A secret or a small memory buys information. **Crispin**, a shabby tanuki-like fey pedlar who sells lost buttons, is friendly and knows that "names go to the Auction House at the end of the street".
- Arcana DC 13: names are stored in small glass bells; Liss's will ring with her tune if the hero hums it.

### 3. The Hall of Mirrors
Before the Auction House lies a corridor of mirrors that shows reflections of what visitors fear to lose. The hero must walk through without looking away (Wisdom save DC 12; failure: frightened for 10 minutes and a mirror double steps out — use Shadow stats, 12 HP, it copies the hero's appearance and vanishes if the hero names something they truly value).

### 4. The Auction House
A small theatre with velvet seats full of masked fey. The **Hollow Auctioneer** (tall, elegant, face like porcelain, voice of honey) sells names one by one. Liss's name is lot 7.
Ways to win it:
- **Bid**: the Auctioneer accepts unusual payments — a true secret from the hero's past, a vivid memory (the hero forgets it permanently), or their own name for one year and a day (a dangerous hook!).
- **Contest**: the Auctioneer loves games. A riddle contest (three riddles; Intelligence DC 13 each, or the player answers in character), a musical duel (Performance DC 14), or a game of chance (roll off against him with a d20; he cheats on a natural 1–5 unless the hero spots it with Perception DC 15).
- **Trick**: fey keep deals to the letter. A clever wording ("I'll pay you what the name is worth to its owner") can beat him. Reward cleverness.
- **Theft**: Sleight of Hand DC 16 to swap the glass bell for a fake. If caught, rule 2 applies with extra menace; the hero must flee through the market with two **Blink Dog**-like fey hounds (AC 13, 11 HP, Bite +3, 1d6+1) in pursuit.

### 5. Dawn
The lanterns start going out one by one. The hero must reach the street's end before the last one does (a skill challenge: three successes before two failures, using Athletics, Acrobatics, Persuasion to get a pixie to hold a lantern, etc.). Failure: the hero is trapped until the next market, unless Crispin guides them out in exchange for a favour "to be named later".

## NPCs
- **Liss Marrow** — kind, dreamy, fading; she flickers in and out of the hero's perception.
- **The Hollow Auctioneer** — courteous, cruel in the way of a cat, delighted by a worthy opponent.
- **Crispin** — scruffy pedlar, lonely, wants a friend; can become a recurring ally.
- **Mother Umber** — grandmotherly, sells weather, can bottle a storm for the hero if paid.
- **Pickle and Wick** — mischievous pixies who talk over each other.

## Encounters
- Mirror double (Shadow stats) — avoidable.
- Fey hounds ×2 — only if the hero steals or draws steel.
- The dawn escape skill challenge.

## Secrets & Clues
- Names can only be bought back with something of equal "weight" to the person who owns them — which means something the hero truly values.
- The Auctioneer has lost his own name long ago and envies mortals. Offering to find it (a future adventure hook) is a bargain he can't refuse.
- Crispin owes the Auctioneer a debt and could be freed by a clever deal.

## Possible Endings
- **Name returned**: the hero rings Liss's bell; she remembers herself and everyone remembers her. She writes a song about the hero. The hero may have paid a price (a memory, a year of their name) — acknowledge it.
- **A bargain too far**: the hero wins the name but owes the Auctioneer a favour. The epilogue should hint at the debt.
- **Trapped**: the hero stays in the market until next month, a guest of the fey — bittersweet but not fatal.
