## Overview
**Cinderwhisk** is a **red dragon wyrmling** (SRD), barely three years old, abandoned by its mother and desperate to build a hoard worthy of a "real" dragon. It is arrogant and dangerous, but also young, lonely and insecure. It has taken over **Old Watch**, a crumbling tower above Stonemere, and extorts the village for gold. Last week it roasted a shepherd's flock when payment was late.

A single level 3 hero cannot easily out-fight a wyrmling, and the adventure is built so that negotiation, trickery or a clever trap are the best routes — though a well-prepared fight is possible.

## Hook
The hero arrives in Stonemere on the day tribute is due. **Mayor Hilde Brask** is scraping coins from the chapel poor-box. She begs the hero to deal with the dragon "by any means" and offers the village's last 60 gp and a sturdy mountain pony.

## Scenes

### 1. Stonemere
A village of slate roofs and stone walls high in a pine valley, with goats everywhere. The sky smells of snow and faint smoke.
- **Mayor Hilde Brask** — stern, exhausted; she'll join the hero in negotiations if asked.
- **Old Fen**, the shepherd who lost his flock, wants the dragon dead.
- **Arla**, a 12-year-old who has been secretly leaving the dragon her old toys, says "it's just lonely". She knows the dragon loves shiny things and stories.
- History DC 12 or asking around: the tower has a sealed dwarven cellar that nobody has opened in a century.

### 2. The climb
A switchback path up the scree to Old Watch. Hazards:
- A rockslide (Dexterity save DC 12, 2d6 bludgeoning, half on success).
- A pair of **wolves** (SRD, 11 HP each) scavenging the roasted flock — they flee if one is badly hurt.
- The path passes beneath the tower's windows; Stealth DC 14 to approach without the dragon noticing (success means the hero can set up a trap or enter unseen).

### 3. Old Watch
A round tower with a collapsed roof, the floor carpeted with coins, cutlery, polished pebbles, goat bells and Arla's toys. **Cinderwhisk** lounges on top. He has a vain, theatrical voice and calls himself "Cinderwhisk the Terrible, Scourge of the Valley".
- He is always willing to talk first — he loves an audience.
- Insight DC 13: he is frightened of the approaching winter and of larger dragons; his mother left him.
- Things Cinderwhisk wants: a hoard that looks impressive, respect, stories about himself, warmth for the winter.
- Bargain options: make him the village's "protector" in exchange for tribute of food instead of gold; tell him about the sealed dwarven cellar (a better lair); flatter him into a truce (Persuasion DC 15; advantage if the hero brings a gift or a story he likes); trick him (Deception DC 15) with fake treasure maps, etc.

### 4. The dwarven cellar
Beneath the tower, a stone door carved with runes (Arcana or History DC 13 to read: "Speak the name of the mountain and enter"). The mountain's name, **Kharzul**, is carved on the village's old boundary stone or known to Old Fen.
- Inside: a warm, dry vault heated by volcanic vents — a perfect dragon lair — plus an old dwarven treasure (a +1 shield or +1 weapon suitable for the hero, 120 gp, a mithral goblet).
- Guarded by a **stone guardian** (use Animated Armor-like stats: AC 18, 25 HP, Slam +4, 1d6+2 ×2) that deactivates if the rune-word is spoken again inside.

### 5. The confrontation (if talks fail)
**Cinderwhisk (Red Dragon Wyrmling)**: AC 17, HP 75 (use 45 for a solo game), flying 60 ft; Rend +6, 1d10+4 slashing plus 1d6 fire; **Fire Breath** (recharge 5–6): 15-ft cone, DC 13 Dex save, 7d6 fire (use 5d6 for a solo game), half on success.
- He flees when reduced to half HP, promising revenge — unless the hero offers mercy and a deal.
- Environment: loose coins (difficult terrain), a collapsing floor, a bell rope the hero can use to swing to the rafters.

## NPCs
- **Cinderwhisk** — red wyrmling; theatrical, vain, lonely; loves compliments and shiny things.
- **Mayor Hilde Brask** — iron-willed; would rather lose the gold than lose a person.
- **Old Fen** — grieving shepherd; will be angry at any deal, but can be won over by compensation.
- **Arla** — the dragon's secret friend; brave and imaginative.

## Encounters
- Rockslide hazard, wolves ×2.
- Stone guardian in the cellar.
- Cinderwhisk (ideally talked down, not fought).

## Secrets & Clues
- The dragon's mother abandoned him because he was the runt of the clutch.
- The dwarven cellar is exactly the lair a young red dragon dreams of.
- Cinderwhisk has never actually hurt a person — only animals — and is secretly horrified by the thought.

## Possible Endings
- **The Dragon of Stonemere**: Cinderwhisk agrees to guard the valley from bandits and bigger monsters in exchange for food, stories and the cellar. The village thrives and Arla gets a dragon for a friend. The hero is paid and gains a dragon ally.
- **Driven off**: the dragon flees, wounded, vowing vengeance — the village is safe for now.
- **Slain**: a heroic victory, but Arla weeps, and the hero finds the dragon's hoard is mostly goat bells.
