## Overview
Sixty years ago the clifftop chapel of Gullstone slid into the sea in a storm. Its bell went down with it. Three weeks ago, a greedy salvager named **Corrin Vale** dived the wreck and pried a silver reliquary from the altar. The reliquary held the bones of Saint Maren, who had bound a **sea hag's curse** to the chapel. With the reliquary gone, the old curse is waking: the drowned bell rings at every high tide and draws the village's children (and the weak-willed) into the water.

The hero can end the curse by returning the reliquary to the drowned chapel, or by destroying the hag's charm that is waking the bell. The hag herself is not present — her curse is — which keeps the threat within reach of a single level 1 hero.

## Hook
The hero arrives in Gullstone at dusk, as a crowd drags a sleepwalking girl (**Pip**, age 9) back from the surf. **Elder Brannoc**, the village headman, offers 50 gp and free lodging for anyone who can "silence that cursed bell". The Salt Cellar tavern is half empty — the boats haven't sailed in a week.

## Scenes

### 1. Gullstone at dusk
Grey stone cottages, nets drying, gulls, the smell of brine and woodsmoke. The bell rings at high tide (around midnight) — a muffled, underwater toll that everyone can feel in their teeth.
- **Elder Brannoc** (tired, proud) gives the history and the reward.
- **Pip's mother, Ennis** (frightened, fierce) says Pip hums a hymn in her sleep that she never learned.
- A DC 12 Insight check on the villagers reveals they suspect Corrin Vale, "who's been flush with coin lately".
- Move on when the hero visits the Salt Cellar, Corrin's shack, or the cliffs.

### 2. The Salt Cellar
Low-beamed tavern; the landlady **Mother Gorse** (gossip, generous with ale) knows everyone's business.
- Corrin sold a silver candlestick in the nearby town last week. He's drinking in the corner, jumpy, and wearing a new coat.
- Persuasion or Intimidation DC 13 gets Corrin to confess and hand over the reliquary's key. Failure: he bolts out of the back door (Athletics DC 12 to catch him on the cliff path).
- If the hero is kind, Corrin admits he still has the reliquary hidden in his shack, and that "it hums at high tide".

### 3. Corrin's shack
A tarred hut above the tideline. The reliquary is hidden under the floorboards (Investigation DC 12, or Corrin shows the hero).
- The reliquary is a small silver casket engraved with waves and a woman holding a bell. Inside: finger bones, a wax seal broken. Religion DC 11: this is a saint's relic that binds something.
- As the hero finds it, **2 drowned dead** (use Zombie stats with 11 HP each, dripping seaweed) shamble up from the beach, drawn by the relic. They are the chapel's old congregation. Alternatively, if the hero reached here fast, only 1 comes.

### 4. The cliffs and the wreck
At low tide, the collapsed chapel's roof is visible below the cliffs. A narrow goat path leads down (Acrobatics DC 10 or slip for 1d6 bludgeoning).
- **Old Hessa**, a half-blind net-mender who lives in a cave at the bottom, remembers the night the chapel fell. She says Saint Maren "sang the sea witch to sleep and put the song in the bell". She can teach the hero the saint's hymn — the same one Pip hums.
- To reach the chapel's altar the hero must dive about 20 feet at low tide (Athletics DC 12 each minute of swimming; a failed check costs 1 level of exhaustion from the cold water, recovered on a long rest). A rope from the villagers, or a potion or spell, gives advantage.

### 5. The drowned chapel (climax, at midnight high tide or low tide)
Inside the flooded nave: pews tangled in kelp, the great bell hanging askew from a broken beam, pale lights drifting in the water. On the altar, an empty niche for the reliquary.
- The **hag's charm**: a knot of black hair, fish-bones and a drowned sailor's ring, woven around the bell's clapper. Arcana DC 12 identifies it as the thing waking the bell. It can be cut away (it has AC 10, 8 HP, and every hit on it causes the bell to toll: DC 11 Wisdom save or be charmed and swim towards the deep for one round).
- A **guardian**: one **Shadow** (SRD) formed from the hag's malice, which attacks anyone disturbing the charm. In a solo game, give it 12 HP.
- Returning the reliquary to the niche while singing the hymn silences the bell at once and banishes the Shadow — the "clever" solution.

## NPCs
- **Elder Brannoc** — grey whiskers, speaks slowly, proud of the village, ashamed to be asking an outsider for help.
- **Ennis** — Pip's mother; brusque, desperate, will join the hero down the cliff if asked (she can hold a rope, but won't fight).
- **Corrin Vale** — salvager, weaselly, ashamed; not evil, just broke. He can be redeemed.
- **Mother Gorse** — tavern keeper, loves a story, knows every rumour.
- **Old Hessa** — ancient, half-blind, cheerful, speaks to the gulls. The only one who remembers Saint Maren.

## Encounters
- Drowned dead ×1–2 (Zombie, 11 HP each).
- Shadow guardian (12 HP) in the chapel.
- Hazards: cliff path, cold-water swim, charm-bell pulses.

## Secrets & Clues
- Corrin took the reliquary; the bell started ringing the next high tide.
- Pip's hymn is Saint Maren's hymn; singing it near the bell calms the drowned dead (they stop and weep).
- The bell rings only at high tide; at low tide the chapel is reachable with a shorter swim.
- The sea hag is long gone (or long dead); only her curse remains. Hint at her with seaweed-woven charms in the village and old carvings.

## Possible Endings
- **Saint restored**: the reliquary is returned and the hymn sung. The bell rings one last, clear note at dawn, heard above water. The village throws a feast; Elder Brannoc pays 50 gp and gives the hero a carved whalebone charm (the villagers swear it keeps you from drowning).
- **Charm destroyed**: the bell falls silent, but the drowned dead are left uneasy. The reward is paid, but Old Hessa warns the sea "remembers".
- **Failure**: if the hero flees or falls, Corrin (redeemed or not) tries to finish the job; the ending is bittersweet.
